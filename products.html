The Tiff platform

Three parts, designed to work together.

Tiff's product architecture moves from conversation to execution, with a team-oriented layer for work at a larger scale.

Product note This page describes the intended architecture. Current availability, exact feature scope and access are not confirmed here.

Assistant layer

Tiffbot

The conversational center of the platform, designed to bring everyday AI tasks into one place.

  • ChatA place to ask, draft, plan and think through a task in conversation.
  • ResearchResearch support intended to help gather information and make a topic easier to work through.
  • ImagesImage creation as part of the assistant experience.

Execution environment

Super Computer

A capability layer in the Tiff architecture for tasks that call for more than conversation.

  • BrowseDesigned for navigating websites and gathering information as part of a task.
  • Code and runA place to write and execute code, then check the result.
  • Work with filesFile-based work and website building are part of the described direction.

Team layer

TiffTeams

A team-scale direction for organizing AI work around clear roles and shared goals.

  • RolesOrganize AI employees around specific responsibilities.
  • ToolsAssociate the right tools with the work each role is intended to handle.
  • TasksStructure work as tasks that can be assigned across a team.
