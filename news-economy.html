Method

The GDP growth calculation is:

growth = (GDP2024 − GDP2022) ÷ GDP2022 × 100

annualised rate = (GDP2024 ÷ GDP2022)1/2 − 1

County 047's recorded GCP is 5.60 times County 032's value and represents 84.86% of the combined value of the two supplied county observations. That comparison is not a ranking of all counties.
