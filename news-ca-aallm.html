Findings

CA-AALLM is Trendify Labs' research programme for culturally aware African large language models. The supplied culture dataset provides a structured context layer with five community records, two language families, five language codes and 15 unique county associations.

Bantu records account for 54.47% of the supplied recorded population and Nilotic records account for 45.53%. These figures describe dataset composition; they are not model accuracy scores or claims about language capability.

Method

The programme connects a human and contextual layer with a model layer. Community, language, subgroup and county fields describe context for research; TAM is the model programme emerging from this work.

coverage ratio = records satisfying a defined context field ÷ total records × 100

In this supplied dataset, all five records include a community name, language code, language family, county associations, population value and source citation. Completeness does not establish representativeness or model performance.

Limitations and interpretation

This release is an exploratory analysis of the supplied dataset, not a complete national or sector-wide estimate. The recorded source citations and archive references should be independently verified before the results are used for policy, production model training or public claims. Percentages describe this dataset only, and do not represent model performance unless explicitly stated.
