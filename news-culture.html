Findings

The supplied culture dataset contains 17,691,808 recorded people across five community records and two language families.

The largest recorded group is Kikuyu at 46.06% of this dataset, followed by Luo at 28.64%. Bantu records account for 9,636,668 people and Nilotic records account for 8,055,140.

Method

Each record includes a community identifier, subgroup, ISO 639-3 language code, language family, primary counties, population value and source citation. The share calculation is:

dataset share = community population ÷ total recorded population × 100

The dataset includes 15 county associations across the five records. This is a structured context layer for AI research, not a complete representation of Kenya's population, communities or languages.

Limitations and interpretation

This release is an exploratory analysis of the supplied dataset, not a complete national or sector-wide estimate. The recorded source citations and archive references should be independently verified before the results are used for policy, production model training or public claims. Percentages describe this dataset only, and do not represent model performance unless explicitly stated.
