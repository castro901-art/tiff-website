Our process

From research to deployment

ResearchStudy the language, culture, context or problem the system is intended to serve.
TrainingDevelop and train models locally using the relevant research and data.
EvaluationTest capabilities, limitations and safety before deployment.
DeploymentIntegrate the resulting models into Trendify systems and products.

Research

Trendify Labs is where our research into contextual AI takes place.

Our research programme includes CA-AALLM, the culturally aware African large language model effort. TAM (Tiff Advanced Models) is the model programme emerging from this work.

Research spans language, culture, economy, sports and governance, with work involving communities and languages across Kenya and Tanzania.

Responsible AI

Our commitments as we research, develop and deploy AI.

SafetyWe evaluate our models before deployment and communicate known limitations as part of our releases.
ContextWe consider the cultural, linguistic and social context in which our systems are developed and used.
CommunityCommunities involved in our research are treated as research partners, with respect for their knowledge, language and culture.
TransparencyWe publish research, model information and release material as our work develops.
PrivacyWe provide information about how data is handled through our privacy documentation.

Our journey

From an early digital venture to an AI research and technology company.

  • 2022Trendify begins as TrendsInfluence, a digital marketing company.
  • 2024The company begins its transition toward AI research and intelligent systems.
  • 2026Trendify operates as an AI research and technology company, with Trendify Labs at the centre of its research work.
  • 4 July 2026Trendify releases Llull, its fast model for structured knowledge.
  • 7 July 2026Trendify releases Aurelius, its model for reasoning and complex knowledge work.
