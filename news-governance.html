Findings

The supplied governance dataset records reported crimes rising from 69,645 in 2020 to 88,415 in 2024.

IndicatorYearScopeObserved value
Crimes reported to police2020National69,645

County own-source revenue2024County 047KSh 12,450.5m

The absolute change is 18,770 reported cases and the calculated increase is 26.95%. The dataset also includes KSh 12,450.5 million in county own-source revenue for county 047 in 2024.

Method

The change calculation is:

percentage change = (2024 value − 2020 value) ÷ 2020 value × 100

These observations can support structured governance analysis, but reported crime counts are not equivalent to total crime incidence, and a two-point comparison cannot establish causation.

Limitations and interpretation

This release is an exploratory analysis of the supplied dataset, not a complete national or sector-wide estimate. The recorded source citations and archive references should be independently verified before the results are used for policy, production model training or public claims. Percentages describe this dataset only, and do not represent model performance unless explicitly stated.
