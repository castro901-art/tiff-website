Culturally Aware African Large Language Models

Large language models have become increasingly capable at processing and generating human language. Yet capability is not distributed equally across languages, cultures and communities.

African languages remain significantly underrepresented in language technologies, while existing evaluation frameworks can fail to capture culturally specific knowledge, social context and forms of expression. Recent research has demonstrated measurable performance gaps across African languages and cultural settings, including in multilingual and multimodal question answering.

This creates a deeper problem than language coverage. A model may speak a language without understanding the world in which that language is used.

CA-AALLM, or Culturally Aware African Large Language Models, is Trendify Labs’ research programme addressing this problem. The programme investigates how African linguistic, cultural and community knowledge can become part of the design, training, evaluation and deployment of intelligent systems.

CA-AALLM is composed of two connected research areas: TAM, the model layer, and Communities, the human and contextual layer.

Tiff Advanced Models

Building language intelligence for African contexts

The performance of large language models is strongly influenced by the quantity, quality and diversity of the data available for training and evaluation. For many African languages, these resources remain comparatively limited.

Recent multilingual and multimodal research has demonstrated significant performance differences between English and African-language settings, including substantial weaknesses in native-language and speech-based cultural question answering. TAM, or Tiff Advanced Models, is Trendify Labs’ research programme focused on this problem.

TAM investigates how advanced language models can be developed for African linguistic and cultural environments without treating those environments as a secondary adaptation layer.

Communities

Building AI with the people it is intended to serve

AI systems are frequently described as technical systems built from data. But data comes from people.

When communities are poorly represented in datasets, the resulting systems can reproduce gaps, stereotypes and misunderstandings. Recent African AI research has increasingly emphasized community participation and culturally grounded evaluation as important components of responsible language technology.

Trendify Labs' Communities research investigates how communities can become participants in the development and evaluation of AI rather than passive sources of training data.

Culture

Teaching machines that context changes meaning

Artificial intelligence increasingly mediates communication, education, information access and decision-making. Yet cultural context is often treated as supplementary information rather than a fundamental component of meaning.

This creates a problem: an AI system can produce linguistically fluent output while misunderstanding the cultural environment in which that output will be interpreted. Trendify Labs’ Culture research investigates how cultural context can be represented, evaluated and incorporated into intelligent systems.

Sports

Building intelligence that understands sport

Sports analytics has transformed how performance is measured. Yet the modern sporting environment produces information across far more dimensions than traditional statistics capture.

Performance is influenced by tactics, opponents, competition, environment, history and human decision-making. Trendify Labs’ Sports research investigates how AI can reason across these dimensions.

Governance

Building intelligence for complex institutions

Governments and public institutions increasingly operate within data-rich environments. Yet more information does not automatically produce better governance.

Public institutions must interpret laws, policies, regulations, budgets, administrative processes and citizen needs while operating within complex institutional constraints. At the same time, African governments are developing AI strategies while facing persistent challenges around infrastructure, institutional capacity, implementation and governance.

Trendify Labs’ Governance research investigates how AI can support institutional intelligence without removing human accountability from governance.
