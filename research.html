Model development

From contextual research to capable systems.

Our model work focuses on what systems understand, what they can reason over and how reliably they can serve their intended context.

Evaluation is part of the research process: we study performance, latency, limitations and safety rather than treating a model as finished when it can generate an answer.

Why context matters

Capability is not distributed equally.

Large language models can process and generate human language, but their performance varies across languages, cultures and communities.

An AI system can be linguistically fluent while misunderstanding the world in which a language is used. Our research treats that context as part of the model problem.
