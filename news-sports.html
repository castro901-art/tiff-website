Findings

The supplied sample contains four FKF Premier League matches and 14 total goals, an average of 3.5 goals per match.

Two matches were home wins and two were away wins. There were no draws in this sample. The largest recorded margin was six goals, in Kariobangi Sharks' 6–0 win over Sofapaka.

Method

Outcome is derived from the score:

home win if home score > away score; away win if away score > home score; otherwise draw.

Goals per match are calculated as home score plus away score. The sample combines two 2024–25 matchweek-one records with two 2025–26 matchweek-34 records, so it is descriptive rather than a season-wide performance estimate.

Limitations and interpretation

This release is an exploratory analysis of the supplied dataset, not a complete national or sector-wide estimate. The recorded source citations and archive references should be independently verified before the results are used for policy, production model training or public claims. Percentages describe this dataset only, and do not represent model performance unless explicitly stated.
