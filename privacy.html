Trendify

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Introduction

Trendify ("we", "our", or "us") is an artificial intelligence research and technology company focused on AI models, intelligent systems and AI infrastructure.

We recognise the importance of protecting personal information and are committed to handling user data responsibly, transparently and securely.

This Privacy Policy explains how Trendify collects, uses, processes, stores, shares and protects personal information when users access our websites, applications, AI systems, products and related services.

By using our services, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal and Account Information

  • Name and username.
  • Email address.
  • Account credentials and authentication information.
  • Profile information.
  • Contact and communication details.

2.2 User-Generated Information

  • Messages and conversations submitted to our AI systems.
  • Files, documents, images and other materials uploaded by users.
  • Prompts, instructions and feedback.
  • Information submitted through forms and support channels.

2.3 Technical Information

  • IP addresses.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Operating system and device identifiers.
  • Usage activity and interaction information.
  • Session identifiers and technical diagnostic information.
  • Security and server access records.

2.4 Transactional Information

Where applicable, we may process transaction references, subscription information, payment status and billing-related records. Payment credentials may be handled by third-party payment service providers.

We do not intentionally collect information beyond what is reasonably necessary for the relevant service or purpose.

3. How We Use Information

Trendify may use collected information to:

  • Create and administer user accounts.
  • Provide access to AI models, assistants and intelligent systems.
  • Process user requests and generate AI responses.
  • Maintain and improve service functionality.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Manage subscriptions and payments.
  • Monitor system performance and reliability.
  • Detect fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Communicate important service updates.
  • Improve the safety, reliability and performance of our technology.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with the purposes disclosed to users unless permitted by applicable law.

4. AI Conversations and User Content

When users interact with Trendify's AI services, their prompts, messages, uploaded materials and related interaction information may be processed to deliver the requested services.

Depending on the service and its underlying technical infrastructure, processing may involve Trendify-operated systems and authorised third-party AI technology providers.

Trendify works with external AI technology providers and infrastructure partners where necessary to deliver its services.

User content may be processed by these providers under applicable contractual and data protection arrangements.

We do not claim ownership of users' original content merely because it is submitted through our services.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or other highly confidential information unless specifically required by a service and appropriate safeguards are provided.

Where information is transferred between jurisdictions or made accessible to authorised service providers in other countries, Trendify will apply appropriate legal, organisational and contractual safeguards as required by applicable law.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Trendify may work with third-party providers to support its technology and business operations.

These may include:

  • Website and domain hosting providers.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers.
  • AI model and API providers.
  • Email and communication providers.

These providers may process information on our behalf or provide independent services under their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Anonymisation

Trendify retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

These include:

  • Account and registration information.
  • AI conversations and interaction records.
  • Customer support communications.
  • Emails and correspondence.
  • Technical and server logs.
  • Security and authentication records.
  • Transactional information.
  • System backups.

Where applicable, certain user interaction records may be subject to a 30-day anonymisation process.

Anonymisation is intended to remove the ability to identify an individual from the relevant information. Properly anonymised information may be retained for analytical, statistical and service-improvement purposes.

The 30-day provision does not automatically apply to every category of information. Different retention requirements may apply to emails, security logs, backups, financial records and other operational information.

Where information is no longer required, it will be deleted, anonymised or otherwise securely disposed of in accordance with applicable retention procedures and legal requirements.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support:

  • Essential website functionality.
  • User authentication and session management.
  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Website performance monitoring.
  • User experience improvements.
  • Website usage analytics, where implemented.

Users can manage cookies through their browser settings. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or tracking technologies.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Data Security

Trendify applies appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

Our security approach includes appropriate access restrictions, system monitoring, secure infrastructure management and other safeguards relevant to the services provided.

Access to personal information is restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who require it for legitimate operational purposes.

Although we take reasonable measures to protect information, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a personal data breach, Trendify will take appropriate action and make notifications to affected individuals and relevant authorities where required by applicable law.

12. Lawful Basis for Processing

Trendify processes personal information under lawful grounds recognised by applicable data protection legislation.

Depending on the processing activity, these grounds may include:

  • Contractual necessity: Where processing is necessary to provide a requested service or fulfil contractual obligations.
  • Consent: Where an individual has provided valid consent for a specific processing activity.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is necessary for legitimate operational, security or service-improvement purposes and does not override the individual's rights.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is required to comply with applicable laws and regulations.
  • Other lawful grounds: Where recognised and permitted under applicable legislation.

The applicable lawful basis depends on the nature and purpose of each processing activity.
