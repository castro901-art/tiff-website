About Trendify

An AI company based in Nairobi, Kenya.

Trendify is developing Tiff, an AI platform organized around an assistant, an execution environment and a team-oriented layer.

Point of view

Useful should feel clear.

The idea behind Tiff is a simpler relationship with AI: describe the work in ordinary language, and let the product architecture bring together the right kind of support.

The details of what is available today are still to be confirmed. This site focuses on the product direction rather than making launch or performance promises.

Clarity in the interface

Keep the product legible, with a clear distinction between conversation, execution and team workflows.

Context matters

Language and culture are part of the company's stated area of interest, with related work described as ongoing.

Build with care

Capability claims should follow confirmed product details, not stand in for them.

Rooted in place

Nairobi is Trendify's company base. The work is informed by its setting without reducing the product to a location slogan.

Read about the language work in progress.

Tiff Advanced Models, or TAM, is described as an ongoing effort focused on East African language and cultural context.
