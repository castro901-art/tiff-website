Approach

Careful work before broad claims.

Understand context

Language carries history, place and everyday usage. Any useful work starts with understanding that context.

Document what is known

Be clear about what is being researched, what has been built and what remains unfinished.

Respect people and names

Community names and language labels should be reviewed with the people they refer to before public claims are made.

Share progress when confirmed

Specific milestones and model availability belong in updates only when Trendify can verify them.
