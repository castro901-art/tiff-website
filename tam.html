Research at Trendify Labs

Our research includes CA-AALLM, the culturally aware African large language model effort, alongside work across culture, economy, sports and governance.

Research topic

CA-AALLM

CA-AALLM stands for culturally aware African large language model. It is our research into building AI that understands African languages, cultures and contexts, and respects the communities that use them.

Model

TAM

TAM (Tiff Advanced Models) is the model that comes out of CA-AALLM. It covers six communities in Kenya and Tanzania.

Communities

TAM is being built with and for these communities.

Where our research goes

Our research also explores how AI can be applied across these areas.

Research area

Culture

Trendify Labs sees language as the home of culture. We document how communities speak and live, and we build models that reflect it, so AI can serve people in their own words and with respect for their traditions.

Research area

Economy

Trendify Labs positions itself as a builder of practical AI for African businesses. We research how AI that understands local languages and context can help more people and small businesses take part in the digital economy.

Research area

Sports

Trendify Labs explores how AI can support African sport, from coaching and officiating to fans following the game in their own languages.

Research area

Governance

Trendify Labs researches how AI can make public information and services easier to understand and reach, in the languages citizens actually use, while keeping people in charge of decisions.
