Tiff PWA

Get the Tiff app

Tiff is a Progressive Web App. Open the official Tiff web app in your browser, then add it to your device’s home screen for quick access. No APK download is needed.

See install steps ↓

Install Tiff on your device

First open Tiff’s official web-app address over HTTPS in a supported browser. Then follow the steps for your device.

Android

Chrome

Open Tiff in Chrome. Tap the browser menu, then choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” and confirm.

iPhone or iPad

Safari

Open Tiff in Safari, tap the Share button, choose “Add to Home Screen,” then tap “Add.”

Desktop

Chrome or Edge

Open Tiff in your browser and select the install icon in the address bar, or choose “Install” from the browser menu.

The exact option can vary by device and browser. If you do not see an install option, use the browser’s “Add to Home Screen” choice where available.
